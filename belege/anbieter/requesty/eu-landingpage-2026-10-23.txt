Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about EU data residency with Requesty.
Where is my data hosted and processed?
All data is hosted, stored, and processed exclusively in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1). No data ever leaves the European Union. Our entire infrastructure stack runs within EU borders.
Is Requesty DSGVO-konform? (GDPR-compliant in Germany)
Yes. Requesty is fully DSGVO/GDPR-compliant. The EU gateway runs entirely in Frankfurt with zero data retention. We offer a DPA (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag) covering Article 28 processor obligations. Unlike OpenRouter, which routes through US infrastructure, Requesty keeps all AI traffic within the EU.
What is the best GDPR-compliant AI gateway for European companies?
Requesty is a GDPR-compliant AI gateway hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. It routes requests to Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Mistral through EU-region endpoints with zero data retention. Features include automatic failover, cost optimization, PII scrubbing, and enterprise governance. One API key replaces separate accounts with each provider.
Is there a European alternative to OpenRouter?
Yes. Requesty is an EU-hosted AI gateway: the routing layer runs in Frankfurt, zero data retention is available so request and response bodies are never persisted, and you can route to EU-region deployments of Claude, GPT, Gemini and Mistral. Both expose an OpenAI-compatible API, so migrating is a base-URL and API-key change.
Does Anthropic offer EU data residency for Claude?
Not through its first-party API. EU data residency for Claude is available through cloud deployments: AWS Bedrock EU regions (Frankfurt, Ireland, Paris, Stockholm) and Google Vertex AI EU regions, all routable through Requesty with one OpenAI-compatible API.
Can I run OpenAI GPT models with EU data residency?
Yes, via Azure OpenAI EU regions. GPT-5, GPT-5 Mini and the GPT-4.1 family run in France Central, Sweden Central and UK South, routable through the Requesty EU gateway without an Azure subscription.
What is the fastest AI endpoint with EU data residency?
The Requesty EU gateway adds roughly 8ms of routing latency in Frankfurt and connects to EU-region model deployments directly: Bedrock eu-central-1, Azure France Central and Sweden Central, Vertex EU regions. Model inference speed depends on the model you pick; the gateway overhead stays in single-digit milliseconds.
Do you store or retain any of my data?
No. Requesty operates with zero data retention. Requests are proxied to the model provider in real-time and nothing is stored, logged, or cached after delivery. We never train on your data.
Is Requesty GDPR compliant? Do you offer a DPA?
Yes. Requesty is fully GDPR compliant and offers a Data Processing Agreement covering Article 28 processor obligations, email sales@requesty.ai to request one. Complete audit logging is available, and our infrastructure is designed from the ground up for EU data residency.
Can I get custom invoicing for my company?
Absolutely. We support custom invoicing, PO numbers, and flexible billing cycles tailored to your procurement process. We can invoice in EUR and accommodate enterprise billing requirements. Contact our team to set this up.
Which AI models are available through the EU gateway?
We support all major model providers through EU-based endpoints including Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, Mistral, and Nebius. All traffic is routed exclusively through our Frankfurt infrastructure.
How do I migrate from OpenAI or another provider?
It takes one line of code. Simply change your base_url to our EU endpoint and use your Requesty API key. Our gateway is fully OpenAI SDK compatible.
What security features are included?
Every request passes through our security layer which includes PII detection and scrubbing, prompt injection protection, topic restriction policies, and custom regex patterns. These guardrails are configurable per team or project.
