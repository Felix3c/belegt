The data is also backed up in compliance with GDPR. STACKIT thus offers a reliable European supplement to the major global cloud providers. Servers in Germany and Austria Security and data protection are at the heart of our products. All our data centers and servers are located in Germany and Austria and are therefore subject to the highest data protection standards that apply worldwide. GDPR-compliant development and operation The development of our cloud takes place exclusively in Germany. All our services are operated in our own data centers. This means that the STACKIT Cloud is 100% compliant with the European General Data Protection Regulation. Processing of data in Germany Processes such as the collection, storage and processing of data in the STACKIT Cloud take place exclusively in our own German data centers. We therefore guarantee the highest security standards and EU data protection regulations. C5 certificate Technical and industrial standards in cloud technology C5 of the BSI, ISAE 3000 (SOC 2) and ISAE 3402 certified The C5 criteria catalog (Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalog) of the German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) defines minimum requirements for secure cloud computing. The STACKIT infrastructure is certified according to C5 Type 2 and thus fulfills one of the highest standards in the area of cybersecurity. Thanks to the additional ISAE 3000 certification (SOC 2) and the ISAE 3402 certificate, international compliance and security requirements are also met.
