How can I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, contact support. Include your account email and team name in the request.

Before requesting deletion:

Pay any outstanding balance on your account.

Download any data you need — account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Revoke all active API keys, or they will be automatically invalidated on deletion.

Account deletion typically takes up to 5 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once the process is complete.

If you need to download your account data before deleting, see How can I download my account information?.
