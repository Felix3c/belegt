How can I download my account information?

To download your account information, contact support. Include the email address associated with your account.

Your download will include:

Account profile data (name, email, team membership).

API usage history and conversation data.

Billing records.

Data download requests are typically fulfilled within 5 business days. You will receive an email with instructions when your data is ready.
