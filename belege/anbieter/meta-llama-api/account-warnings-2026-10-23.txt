Why did I receive a warning or enforcement action on my account?

Account warnings and enforcement actions are issued when the system detects activity that may violate the Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy.

What to expect

Warnings require you to share the measures you are taking to prevent future violations.

Enforcement actions may include suspension of your API key or blocking of API access.

Failure to respond to a warning, or continued violations, may result in escalating enforcement.

Details about the specific violation and your options are included in the email notification you received.

How to appeal

If you believe the warning or enforcement action was issued in error, contact support.
