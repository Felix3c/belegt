How do I update my team's business information?

Your business information is the legal business name and address used on your team's tax documents. Only admins can edit it.

To update it:

Select Billing in the left navigation.

Open the Business information tab.

Select Edit, update the details, and save.

If your business isn't registered, use your legal name and mailing address. Only team admins can make this change.
