How do I use Contributor mode on Meta Model API?

On Meta Model API, you use Contributor mode by calling a model's Contributor-mode variant, a model ID that ends in -contributor; see Models for which models have one. Contributor-mode variants aren't available to teams with Zero Data Retention (ZDR). For Contributor mode in a Muse Code subscription, see What is Contributor mode in Muse Code?.

You have certain responsibilities while using Discounted Services. Below is a summary, but please carefully read the full Meta Model API Terms of Service.

Do Not Submit Personal Information

You must not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Discounted Services. This means you — and your end users — should not send inputs that contain any personal or confidential information.

If your use case involves processing personal information, please use Standard Services.

Restricted Territories Apply to All Services

There are no Discounted-tier-specific geographic restrictions. The list of Restricted Territories (e.g., Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, China, etc.) applies to all Services — Discounted and Standard alike. See the Meta Model API Geographic Use Policy for the full list.

Do Not Make Integrated Products Available to Users Under 18

You and your end users must be at least 18 years of age to use the Services and any Integrated Products. You must not create Integrated Products targeted at individuals under 18.

Maintain a Privacy Policy

You must provide and adhere to a privacy policy for all of your Integrated Products that clearly and accurately describes to your end users:

What user information you collect

How you use and share that information

Whether information is used for advertising or marketing
