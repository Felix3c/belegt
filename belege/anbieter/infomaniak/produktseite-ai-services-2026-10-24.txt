Do you have any questions or doubts?
We’ve compiled the questions we’re often asked – see below.
Is it possible to train AI Services using custom data?
We don’t currently offer the option of training our AI Services using custom data.
Can I use AI Services to sell services through my company?
Yes, AI Services can be integrated into your apps, which you are free to monetise.
Can I generate or interpret images using AI Services?
The possibility of generating or interpreting images with AI Services is currently being analysed. We will offer you this functionality when it is fully operational and also guarantees total control of your data.
Can I transcribe an audio file into text using AI Services?
Yes, AI Services is a multi-model product that includes Whisper, which allows you to transcribe audio files into text.
How many words can AI Services process per prompt?
Our proposed LLM templates have different limitations due to variations in the complexity and size of the templates. However, you can find the maximum prompts size accepted by our templates here
What is a token?
Tokens are units that divide up the text before the API analyses it, either as characters or as a set of words. In general, a token corresponds to 4 characters in English or 3/4 of a word, depending on the language.
What's the difference between an incoming and an outgoing token?
Incoming tokens are requests made to the AI, and outgoing tokens are responses generated by the AI.
Is Infomaniak's AI connected to the Internet?
No, the models we propose are based on a gigantic dataset, which does not need to be continuously fed with new information.
Which AI model does AI Services use?
We use several open-source models to offer a wide range of tools through AI Services, such as Mixtral for text generation and Whisper for audio transcription.
You can find the full list of models we offer here
How does AI Services billing work?
You can use the AI immediately and track your consumption in real time. You will be billed at the end of the month according to the number of tokens consumed. When you finalise the creation of your account, we'll ask you to confirm your identity by registering a valid payment card, thereby reinforcing our anti-fraud measures.
Does AI Services use OpenAI standards?
Yes, the various models we offer comply with OpenAI standards to facilitate the transition to our AI Services and their use.
How does per-minute billing work?
Transcription or image generation models are billed per minute of generation. This time depends on several parameters, such as the size of the file to be transcribed (for transcription) or the quality of the image and the number of images generated simultaneously (for images).
It is this generation time that is billed. For example, an image generated in 4 seconds using SDXL-Lightning will be billed in proportion to the indicated price, i.e. CHF 0.02.
Prices do not include VAT
