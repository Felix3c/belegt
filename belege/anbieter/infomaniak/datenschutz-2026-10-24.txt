Data confidentiality policy
It is important to distinguish between the personal data you entrust to us for the use of our services, and the security of the data you host on Infomaniak's technical infrastructures.
You'll find all the common questions concerning the security of the data you host, as well as their precise scope, in our space dedicated to security issues.
This confidentiality policy applies to all our products and services.
Data controller: Infomaniak Network SA, Rue Eugène-Marziano 25, 1227 Les Acacias (GE), Switzerland. If you have any questions regarding data protection or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at dpo@infomaniak.com or by mail at the above address, for the attention of the Data Protection Officer.
Commitment to the confidentiality of your personal data
Infomaniak collects as little information about you as possible. In order to be able to deliver your order, guarantee the security of your data or collect your opinion on specific pages, we may nevertheless require certain personal information.
Infomaniak processes your personal data with care and ensures that your privacy is protected. Here are our three commitments regarding the confidentiality of your personal data:
Usage limited as much as possible
We never share your personal data with third parties without a valid reason.
Responsible processing
We protect and treat your personal information as if it were our own.
Transparent use
We use your personal information solely for the purposes described below.
What personal information does Infomaniak store?
Name, address and place of residence
Your name is stored, as you are more than just a customer number to us when you contact our support team. We also need your name and place of residence to prepare your invoices, contact you or visit you for a cup of coffee (if you invite us).
Email address
We store your e-mail address to send you important messages about your order or account. We also keep you informed of new developments at Infomaniak via newsletters. If necessary, you can choose to unsubscribe from these.
Phone number
We store your telephone number to contact you in case of emergency or to ask for your opinion. In addition, we use your number to send you SMS messages for two-step verification or to authenticate you.
Login details and customer number
We store your login details to secure access to your account and ensure that only authorised people can access your personal data. We also generate a unique customer number so we can help you more quickly and efficiently.
IP address
We store your IP address to give you an overview of the IP addresses used to connect to your account and to verify the origin of any changes. This information is also used to strengthen the protection of your account against unauthorised access from other locations.
Payment details
We store payment information such as your PayPal details, your credit card, your account number and your name as the account holder. The processing of your payments also involves other parties, such as companies affiliated with your payment method or credit cards. When you place an order, we also generate a unique offer or an invoice number which is then linked to your account.
Your use of our Internet site
Cookies and similar technologies allow us to understand how our website and services are used so that we can improve them. We track our website’s traffic using Matomo, a software package hosted on our own servers in Switzerland: this data is not shared with any third parties. This tracking is activated solely with your consent, which you can modify at any time using the “Manage your cookies” button at the bottom of the pages. With your consent only, cookies are also used to measure the effectiveness of our ads and to personalise them. For details on how your data is used, please refer to the “Products, Services & Applications” section at the bottom of the page.
When do we share your information with other parties?
Infomaniak develops most of its solutions in-house and works exclusively with partners who meet our security standards. A detailed list of third parties involved in our website, your orders and the security of your account can be found in the “Does Infomaniak share my data with third parties?” section below, along with the other categories of recipients. In summary, your data is shared with third parties primarily in the following cases:
Providing or performing our services
We use services such as Checkout and PayPal to offer different payment options, Twilio to send two-step verification text messages and MaxMind to prevent payment fraud.
Improve our services, our site or our advertising
With your consent, we measure traffic on our website using Matomo, which is hosted on our own servers, and we use our advertising partners' tools to measure the effectiveness of our ads.
Get feedback
We sometimes use survey companies to find out how satisfied you are with our services.
Secure our website and display our customers' reviews
We use ALTCHA and Google reCAPTCHA to secure our forms, as well as Trustindex to display our customers' reviews.
Pay our affiliate partners
With your consent, we use Awin to allocate orders to our affiliate partners and calculate their commissions.
Fulfilling a legal obligation
An authority may compel us, through a decision valid under Swiss law, to disclose data to it.
Register a domain name
ICANN-accredited registrars are obliged to publish the details of the owner of a domain in the Whois database of the registry responsible for the domain. This means that anyone can check the availability of a domain and see who owns it. With Domain Privacy, Infomaniak nevertheless offers the possibility of keeping this data confidential when authorised by the domain registry.

Geolocation: used to cross-reference data with the telephone number code.
Identity documents: used to verify the user's identity.
Selfie: used for user identification.Legal basis: our legitimate interest and that of our customers in preventing identity theft and fraudulent access to accounts. The comparison between the selfie and the ID document is performed by a member of our support team, without automated facial recognition.
How is your personal data shared?With the exception of the phone number, which is shared with our SMS service provider to send the verification code (see “Does Infomaniak share my data with third parties?”), this data is processed by our support team and is not shared with any third parties.
