Data Centers
Where Our Infrastructures Are Located
Regolo.ai's inference infrastructures are physically hosted in data centers strategically distributed across Europe, ensuring low latency, reliability, and compliance with European regulations.
All involved data centers are certified and powered by renewable energy sources, contributing to more sustainable AI infrastructures without compromising performance and operational standards.
🇮🇹
Lazio, Italy
Province of Frosinone, near Rome
A central location in an industrial area that ensures high connectivity to major European networks. This strategic positioning provides optimal routing and minimal latency for inference requests across the continent.
🇮🇹
Lombardy, Italy
One of Europe's leading digital hubs
One of Europe's leading digital and industrial hubs. The Lombardy region offers world-class connectivity infrastructure and access to a vibrant technology ecosystem.
🇪🇺
Ecosystem & DHH S.p.A.
The infrastructures extend through the Caldera ecosystem, further enhancing resilience and performance. The network continues to expand thanks to additional regions provided by DHH S.p.A., a European group specialized in cloud computing and infrastructure services of which Regolo.ai is part, enabling broader geographic workload distribution and increased operational continuity.
