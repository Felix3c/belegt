Frequently Asked Questions
Why is US cloud AI (OpenAI, Anthropic) not enough for European enterprise compliance, even with EU regions enabled?
While OpenAI and Anthropic offer regional data residency, official documentation (October 2026) confirms critical compliance gaps for enterprise buyers: (1) System data (account metadata, usage statistics, support tickets, and structured output schemas) is explicitly excluded from EU residency and processed globally. (2) Sub-processor chains route traffic through foreign entities, leaving parent corporations legally subject to the US CLOUD Act and FISA Section 702 extraterritorial disclosure orders without notifying European clients. (3) Zero Data Retention (ZDR) is available only upon bespoke sales approval and excludes stateful endpoints, with standard logs retained for 30 days. In contrast, Regolo provides 100% in-memory execution in EU facilities (Milan Caldera & Frankfurt) with an independent ZDR audit completed on 24 July 2026.
Can European system integrators and software agencies legally guarantee strict EU data residency in DACH and Benelux under GDPR?
Yes, but only if the underlying inference infrastructure operates strictly within the European Economic Area (EEA). Under GDPR Article 28 and Article 82, European integrators and digital agencies face joint liability if foreign sub-processors route data through overseas jurisdictions. Regolo resolves this requirement by executing all AI workloads strictly in European sovereign data centers in Milan and Frankfurt connected via DE-CIX and AMS-IX (5–12ms latency), providing European partners with audit-ready DPAs, certified Zero Data Retention, and complete immunity from foreign extraterritorial warrants.
How does white-label sovereign AI resale work and what profit margins can integrators achieve?
Integrators deploy AI endpoints under their own brand, domain (e.g. api.your-brand.co.uk/v1), and client invoicing, achieving gross profit margins between 80% and 95%. While US frontier APIs charge up to $20.00–$25.00 per 1M output tokens leaving zero reseller margin, Regolo serves open-weight models (GLM 5.2 at £1.70/£4.40, Qwen 3.8 27B at £0.42/£1.76, and lightweight 9B search models at £0.06/M tokens). For example, a 50,000 monthly search workload costing £46 on Regolo is packaged into a £300/month enterprise search retainer, securing an 84%+ net margin with zero GPU hardware CapEx.
Does Regolo provide audit rights and certifications for DORA Art. 30 and EU AI Act compliance?
Yes. Unlike standard click-wrap API agreements from hyperscalers that limit compliance to self-assessment questionnaires, Regolo provides institutional DPAs with contractual on-site audit rights aligned with DORA Article 30 and FCA/PRA operational resilience rules. The infrastructure holds 12 accredited datacenter certifications, including ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security), ISO 22301 (Business Continuity), ISO 27017 (Cloud Security), ISO 27018 (Cloud Privacy & PII Protection), CSA STAR Level 1, CISPE Code of Conduct, and an independent third-party Zero Data Retention audit (24 July 2026).
