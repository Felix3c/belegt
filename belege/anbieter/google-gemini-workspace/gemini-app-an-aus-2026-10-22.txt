Control how Workspace users access the Gemini app
As an administrator, you control who uses the Gemini app in your
organization. The Gemini app can help users supercharge their ideas.
With a little help from the Gemini app, users can do things like:
Brainstorm ideas, develop a plan, or find different ways to get things done.
Get a quick, easy to understand summary of more complex topics.
Create first drafts of outlines, emails, blog posts, and much more.
Learn more about the Gemini app .
What is the Gemini app?
The Gemini app includes:
The Gemini web app at gemini.google.com
Gemini mobile app on Android and iOS
Gemini in Chrome
Gemini on Mac
Gemini for Windows
"Gemini Apps" are also sometimes referred to as the "Gemini app" or "Gemini."
If you have a work or school Google Account, your use of Gemini Apps may be
subject to different data handling terms. Learn more about
using Gemini Apps with a work or school Google Account .

Gemini limits
Gemini Apps has usage limits designed to ensure an optimal experience for
everyone. This means we may at times have to cap the number of prompts and
conversations you can have, or how much you can use some features, within a
specific timeframe.
The number of prompts you can use before hitting the limit varies and depends on
factors like how long and complex your prompts are, the size and number of files
you upload, and the length of your conversations with Gemini Apps.
Your capacity replenishes regularly, so you can get back to chatting with Gemini
Apps soon, or you can upgrade to a higher plan.

Gemini activity settings
When you use the Gemini web app or mobile app with your work or school
account, conversation history is saved by default for 18 months. Your
administrator can change the auto-delete setting to delete activity after 3,
18, or 36 months or turn history off altogether.
When Keep Activity is on, it includes activity from the Gemini web and mobile
apps. Clearing your Gemini for Google Workspace history from a Workspace app
doesn't affect information saved to Gemini Apps Activity.
Important for work users with access to the Gemini app as an additional
service: When Gemini Apps Activity is on, Google collects your Gemini app
conversations and other information you provide and uses it to improve and
develop Google products, services, and machine-learning technologies.
Learn more .

Manage Gemini conversation history
The Gemini conversation history setting lets you manage whether Gemini
conversations are saved and for how long before they're automatically deleted.
When Gemini conversation history is on or off, chats are used as described in the
Google Workspace Terms of Service .
When Gemini conversation history is off, users won't be able to access previous
conversations. Once they leave a conversation, they won't be able to see it
again. Google may still store these conversations for up to 72 hours so Google
can provide the service. This activity doesn't appear in Gemini Apps Activity.
When you turn the setting on and then off, any existing conversation history is
stored for the length of time set in the Conversation retention option. Gemini
responses based on Google Chat data might be retained longer than the
conversation where the data originated.
Note: When Gemini conversation history is off, Workspace apps integrations
are not accessible to Gemini.
Only Workspace admins have access to the Gemini conversation history setting.
The setting can't be changed by Workspace users. Conversation history is on by
default.
In the Google Admin console, go to Menu
Generative AI
Gemini app .
Go to Gemini app
Requires having the Gemini Settings administrator privilege .
Click Gemini conversation history .
To turn conversation history on or off, select or deselect the Enable
Gemini conversation history box.
When conversation history is on, below Conversation retention , select how
long conversations are stored before they're automatically deleted: 3, 18, or
36 months. The default is 18 months. The minimum conversation retention time
is 3 months.
Note: Depending on settings configured for your organization, users might
be able to delete individual conversations. Learn how to control chat
deletion in the Gemini App .
(Optional) To apply the setting only to some users, at the side, select an organizational unit (often used for departments) or configuration group (advanced).
Group settings override organizational units. Learn more
Click Save .
Changes can take up to 72 hours but typically happen more quickly.
Note: For a newly created user, Gemini conversation history can take up to 72
hours to take effect. Conversations within the first 72 hours after a user has
been created might not be saved in Gemini conversation history.
