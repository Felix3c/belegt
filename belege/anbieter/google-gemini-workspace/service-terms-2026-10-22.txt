1.2 AppSheet primary data storage and processing . Notwithstanding that AppSheet may be included in an In-Scope Edition, the Data Regions Policy will only:
(a) apply to AppSheet where: (i) the Customer is using AppSheet Enterprise Plus; and (ii) an End User has selected specific Customer Data for storage and, if applicable, processing in use in the Data Region; and
(b) cover processing in use of Covered Data if the Customer is using an In-Scope Edition that includes Data Region coverage for processing of data in use.

For clarity (a) Google may store or process any Customer Data that is not covered by the Data Regions Policy wherever Google or its Subprocessors maintain facilities, and (b) the Services do not limit the locations from which the Customer or End Users may access Customer Data or to which they may move it.

'Covered Data' means the specific Customer Data identified at the Workspace Data Regions Page as covered by Data Regions.
'Covered Service' means the specific Core Services and Other Services identified at the Workspace Data Regions Page as supported for Data Regions.
'Data Region' means:
(a) either the United States or Europe, except in relation to AppSheet; or
(b) Europe, in relation to AppSheet.
'In-Scope Edition' means the specific Google Workspace editions identified at the Workspace Data Regions Page as supported for Data Regions.
'Workspace Data Regions Page' means https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/compliance/data-covered-by-data-regions .
