Manage Gemini in Workspace conversation history settings

Supported editions for this feature: Business Starter, Business Standard, and Business Plus; Enterprise Starter, Enterprise Standard and Enterprise Plus; Education Plus, Teaching & Learning add-on, and Google AI Pro for Education add-on; Frontline Plus. Compare your edition
Conversation history lets users see and continue their past chats with Gemini in
Workspace. As an administrator, you can decide how conversation history works
for your team, including if users can keep or delete their conversations.
Important
This article explains how to manage conversation history for Gemini in
Workspace. To manage history for the Gemini app, use these instructions
instead .
To access Gemini in Workspace services, users need to turn on smart
features and
personalization .
Allow users to delete conversations
This setting determines if users can delete their own conversations with Gemini
in Workspace. If you turn this setting off, users see a message explaining that
their admin has disabled this option.
In the Google Admin console, go to Menu

Gemini for Workspace .
Go to Gemini for Workspace
Requires having the Gemini Settings administrator privilege .
Go to Conversation history & deletion Manual
deletion .
(Optional) To apply the setting only to some users, at the side, select an organizational unit (often used for departments) or configuration group (advanced).
Group settings override organizational units. Learn more
Select On or Off .
Click Save .
Auto-deletion settings
To support your organization's data retention policies, you can choose whether
to automatically delete inactive conversations. Conversations are considered
inactive if messages haven't been added or edited over a set period of time. You
can also allow users to choose their own auto-deletion settings.
Conversations that match your settings are usually deleted within a day, but
processing errors might cause delays.
In the Google Admin console, go to Menu
Generative AI
Gemini for Workspace .
Go to Gemini for Workspace
Requires having the Gemini Settings administrator privilege .
Go to Conversation history & deletion Auto-deletion .
(Optional) To apply the setting only to some users, at the side, select an organizational unit (often used for departments) or configuration group (advanced).
Group settings override organizational units. Learn more
Choose an option:
Let users choose their own setting
Delete after 90 days (~3 months) —Conversations are automatically
deleted 90 days after the last interaction.
Delete after 540 days (~18 months) —Conversations are automatically
deleted 540 days after the last interaction.
Delete after 1080 days (~3 years) —Conversations are automatically
deleted 3 years after the last interaction.
Don't automatically delete —Conversations are retained indefinitely.
Click Save .
