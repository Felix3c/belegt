Qwen3‑235B and OpenAI’s gpt‑oss Models now available on Scaleway’s Generative APIs and Managed Inference
Paris, France, August 6, 2025
– Scaleway, the European AI neocloud, is expanding its catalog of sovereign models with the addition of
Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507
, as well as
gpt‑oss‑120B
and
gpt‑oss‑20B
, two newly released open-weight models from OpenAI.
Qwen3‑235B, developed by Alibaba Cloud, is one of the most advanced open models to date. It ranks in the global top 5 on
LMArena
, with performance comparable to proprietary models such as Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT‑4.5. It delivers strong reasoning capabilities while maintaining low latency and high token efficiency, making it ideal for real-time agentic applications.
OpenAI’s gpt‑oss models mark the company’s first open-weight release since GPT‑2. Licensed under Apache 2.0, they offer high reasoning performance with lighter compute requirements, enabling developers to build cost-efficient, tool-using agents deployed on dedicated infrastructure.
Qwen3‑235B
is now available via
Generative APIs
and
Managed Inference
, while
gpt‑oss‑120B
and
gpt‑oss‑20B
are available on
Managed Inference
. Support for gpt‑oss models on Generative APIs will be added shortly.
As always, all inference runs on Scaleway’s sovereign infrastructure in Europe, and prompts are never stored, logged, or reused, in full compliance with our Data Privacy Policy.
“These additions strengthen our commitment to open AI,”
said Franck Pagny, Product Manager for Inference at Scaleway.
“Developers can now run top-tier open models, with full control, right here in Europe.”
