 Why sovereign AI matters
 European organizations need AI infrastructure they can trust. Here’s why sovereign AI is becoming a business requirement.
 The EU AI Act demands it
 The EU AI Act introduces strict requirements for AI systems operating in Europe. Organizations must ensure transparency, accountability, and data governance for any AI they deploy.
 Data residency is non-negotiable
 Under the GDPR, personal data processed by AI must stay within the EU/EEA. Routing requests through non-EU infrastructure creates compliance risk, even if the data is encrypted.
 Reduce vendor lock-in risk
 Relying on a single non-EU AI provider creates dependency on foreign infrastructure and policy changes. A sovereign AI approach lets you switch providers freely while staying compliant.
 European AI models are catching up
 Models from Mistral, Aleph Alpha, and other European AI providers now compete on quality with US alternatives. Sovereign AI no longer means compromising on capability.
 Integrate AI without GDPR risk.
 You need AI that won’t create compliance headaches. Your data stays in the EU, GDPR is enforced by default, and every request is routed for the best balance of cost, latency, and uptime, reducing risk while improving performance.
