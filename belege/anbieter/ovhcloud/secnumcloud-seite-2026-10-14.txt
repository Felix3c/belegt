Objectives of the SecNumCloud framework In today’s interconnected global environment, digital sovereignty is a major political issue. In 2017, ANSSI published the SecNumCloud framework and a qualification procedure for “cloud computing service providers”, modelled on those existing for trusted service providers (electronic certification, incident detection, timestamping, security audit, etc.). Its aim is to guide administrative authorities, operators of vital importance (OVIs), and all European and French economic operators towards trusted cloud providers.
