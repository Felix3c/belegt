1. zero-data-retention, „Implicit data caching“:
By default, Google's published Gemini models cache Customer Data to reduce latency and accelerate responses. Implicit caching uses two storage tiers:
In-memory caching : Enabled by default for supported Gemini models.
Durable caching : Enabled by default for supported Gemini models.

2. zero-data-retention, „Durable data caching“:
On models that support durable caching, when in-memory cache capacity is exhausted, Google stores derived cache data (key-value representations computed from your inputs) in durable storage within the Agent Platform serving environment.
It's stored at rest and encrypted with Google default encryption.
It's deleted no later than 24 hours after it's written, and is often evicted sooner.
It stays within the selected location and adheres to its Data Residency requirements when you use a regional or multi-regional endpoint. When you use the global endpoint, requests and their cached data can be served from any region.
Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) aren't supported for durable caching.

3. zero-data-retention, „Control data caching“:
If your organization's policy does not permit Customer Data to be stored at rest, you can disable durable caching on a per-project basis and maintain in-memory caching. Alternatively, you can disable caching entirely.

4. abuse-monitoring, „Google Frontier Models“:
Google may log customer prompts and responses solely for ensuring safe use of the Google Frontier Models and compliance with customer's Google agreement
This includes automated processing to create anonymized shingles and analyze usage patterns solely for the purpose described above.
These logs are stored securely inside the customer's environment for up to 30 days.

5. abuse-monitoring, gleich danach:
ZDR-aligned abuse logging for customers with Cloud Master Agreements : Section 34 "Google Frontier Models" of Service Specific Terms does not give Google the right to conduct human review. Consequently, these logs adhere to ZDR standards for customers with Cloud Master Agreements, as they are stored inside the customer's environment and exempt from human review.
