Third-Party Subprocessors
Google and its affiliates engage the third-party entities in the table below to perform limited activities in connection with the Google Cloud Platform Services. The table shows what activity each entity performs and indicates if an entity is only relevant to a specific Service or Region. More information about each activity is provided directly below. This explains the limited processing of Customer Data the entity is authorized to perform.
Data Labeling: Human labelers apply labels to datasets submitted by Customer based on instructions provided by Customer. See the Data Labeling Use Cases page for more information.
Technical Support: Customer-initiated technical support:
in the case of Apigee as described in the Apigee Technical Support Services Guidelines . The Subprocessor only has access to Customer Data if access is enabled in the Admin Console. See the Trace Overview page for more information.
in the case of all other Google Cloud Platform Services as described in the Google Cloud Platform Technical Support Service Guidelines . The Subprocessor does not have access to Customer Data stored or processed by the Services. The Subprocessor only has access to Customer Data if Customer explicitly elects to share Customer Data in the course of a support case.
Assured Support: Customer-initiated technical support as described in the Google Cloud Platform Technical Support Service Guidelines . If Customer chooses to enable an Assured Workloads control package with a technical support location requirement, then only Subprocessors listed as performing Assured Support for locations permitted under that control package will be engaged to provide technical support to Customer. See the Assured Workloads Getting Support page for more information.
Voice Transcription: Human transcribers label and transcribe audio recordings. This activity only occurs if Customer elects to opt in to the Speech-to-Text data logging program. See the Speech-to-Text Data Logging page for more information.
Managing Hosting Environment : Monitoring, troubleshooting and ongoing management of the hosting environment that stores Customer Data. Subprocessor personnel do not require access to Customer Data to perform this activity. See the Bare Metal Solution overview page for more information.
More information about Third Party Subprocessors for Google Cloud Platform can be found in the Further Information section below.

Further Information about Google Cloud Platform Subprocessors
1. Which GCP Subprocessors are relevant to my organization?
The GCP Subprocessors tables show the following information for each Subprocessor:
Relevant GCP service
Applicable Cloud region
You can use this information to determine which Subprocessors are relevant to your organization.
Some Subprocessors are only relevant to a specific GCP service . For example: Subprocessors performing Voice Transcription are only relevant to the Speech-to-Text service. If your organization is not using the Speech-to-Text service, then these Subprocessors are not relevant to you.
Some Subprocessors are only relevant to a specific Cloud region . For example: Subprocessors performing Data Center Operations are only relevant to a discrete region. If your organization is not using this region, then the Subprocessor is not relevant to you.
Some Subprocessors are relevant to all GCP services in all Cloud regions . For example: Certain Subprocessors performing Technical Support are relevant to all services in all regions. These Subprocessors are relevant to you regardless of which services or regions your organization uses.
2. Why are all GCP Technical Support Subprocessors relevant to my organization regardless of my location?
To provide 24/7 support and meet our robust SLAs, we provide Technical Support based on a global follow-the-sun model. Under this model, customer location does not exclusively determine which support agent is engaged for a support case. This also depends on other factors like:
Time of day: When the ticket is submitted.
Priority: The urgency of the request.
Language: The language in which the request is made.
If you would like support to be provided from specific locations, you can choose to enable an Assured Workloads control package with a technical support location requirement. If you choose to do this, then only Subprocessors listed as performing Assured Support for locations permitted under that control package will be engaged to provide technical support for your organization. See the Assured Workloads Getting Support page for more information.
3. Why are all GCP Technical Support Subprocessors relevant to my organization regardless of the services we are using?
To provide customers with a seamless and uninterrupted support experience, we ensure a majority of our support agents can assist with all GCP services.
This approach enables us to meet our robust SLAs even during busy periods and safeguard business continuity. It also reflects the fact that customers often use our suite of services in combination and addressing a technical issue could require expertise across multiple services.
4. Does Google Cloud provide advance notice of changes to the GCP Subprocessor page?
Yes. Please refer to the “Subprocessor” section of the Cloud Data Processing Addendum for more information about what changes we notify and the applicable notice periods.
5. How does Google Cloud provide notice of changes to the GCP Subprocessor page?
We send notices to the email address(es) your organization has designated in the Admin Console or Order Form to receive notifications from Google. You can further define and manage who receives this notice via the ‘Legal’ category in the Essential Contacts feature.
6. Do I need to subscribe to receive notice of changes to the Subprocessor page?
No. As long as you have designated an email to receive notifications from Google in the Admin Console, an Order Form or via Essential Contacts , you don’t need to subscribe to receive notifications of Subprocessor changes.
