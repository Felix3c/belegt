Log in to download certificates and reports What is TISAX? TISAX (Trusted Information Security Assessment Exchange) is the leading information security assessment framework for companies working with the automotive industry, based on the VDA-ISA standard and governed by the ENX Association. TISAX certification is increasingly required by automotive manufacturers and suppliers to ensure secure handling of sensitive information, intellectual property, and personal data across the automotive value chain. Exoscale is TISAX Level 2 assessed. Our TISAX assessment results and details are available to authorized automotive industry partners via the ENX portal . FAQ What is the TISAX assessment process? What are the TISAX protection levels? Where can I find Exoscale's TISAX results? How does TISAX relate to other standards? What is the TISAX assessment process? The TISAX process is overseen by the ENX Association, which maintains the assessment and exchange framework. The process begins with a self-assessment based on the VDA Information Security Assessment (VDA-ISA). An accredited, independent audit provider is engaged to perform an on-site or remote assessment. Successful completion of the audit results in a TISAX assessment label (Level 1, 2, or 3), valid for a defined period. Results are shared securely with authorized partners via the ENX portal. Exoscale is listed in the ENX portal, making our assessment results easily accessible to automotive industry partners. What are the TISAX protection levels? TISAX defines three protection levels, depending on the sensitivity of information handled: Level 1: Basic protection (self-assessment). Level 2: High protection (audited, suitable for most sensitive business information). Level 3: Very high protection (audited, for highly sensitive/prototype data). Exoscale has achieved TISAX Level 2 , demonstrating high protection of sensitive information and alignment with automotive industry requirements. Where can I find Exoscale's TISAX results? Exoscale’s TISAX assessment results (Level 2) are published on the ENX portal and can be accessed by authorized automotive industry partners and customers. Visit the ENX portal and search for Exoscale to view our assessment details. For more information or to request documentation, contact our compliance team. How does TISAX relate to other standards? TISAX builds on the principles of ISO/IEC 27001, the international standard for information security management. What makes TISAX unique: - It is tailored for the automotive industry, with a focus on protecting prototypes, intellectual property, and personal data. - The VDA-ISA standard provides detailed assessment criteria aligned with automotive supply chain requirements. - TISAX enables secure, standardized sharing of assessment results between suppliers and manufacturers via the ENX portal.
