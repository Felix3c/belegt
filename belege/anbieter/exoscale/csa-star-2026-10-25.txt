publishing our self-assessment using the CAIQ, providing transparency and assurance to cloud customers and partners. Access Compliance Center What is the Cloud Security Alliance STAR Program? The Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) is a global not-for-profit organization dedicated to defining and raising awareness of best practices for secure cloud computing. The CSA STAR (Security, Trust, Assurance, and Risk) program is a globally recognized cloud assurance framework that promotes transparency and best practices for cloud providers. At the heart of STAR is the Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM) —a comprehensive set of security controls mapped to major international standards. The Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ) is a standardized self-assessment tool for cloud providers to document their security controls and practices against the CCM. Exoscale’s completed CAIQ is available to customers and partners in our Compliance Center, and published on the CSA STAR Registry for full transparency. FAQ Who can use the Cloud Controls Matrix? What domains are covered by the CCM? What is the CAIQ and how is it used? How does CSA STAR relate to other standards? Who can use the Cloud Controls Matrix? The CCM is used by cloud service providers to benchmark and improve their security controls, and by cloud customers to assess provider risk, compare services, and satisfy due diligence or regulatory requirements. Both providers and customers benefit from a standardized approach to cloud security, enabling more efficient and transparent vendor assessments. What domains are covered by the CCM? The CCM covers 17 major domains, including: - Identity & Access Management - Application & Interface Security - Data Security & Information Lifecycle Management - Encryption & Key Management - Security Incident Management - Business Continuity Management - Supply Chain Management - Audit & Compliance - And more Each domain maps to hundreds of detailed controls and best practices aligned with international frameworks such as ISO/IEC 27001, NIST, SOC 2, and more. What is the CAIQ and how is it used? The CAIQ (Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire) is a self-assessment tool provided by the CSA. Cloud providers complete the CAIQ to demonstrate how their security controls align with each requirement of the CCM, providing clear answers for customer review. Exoscale’s CAIQ response is available in our Compliance Center and on the CSA STAR Registry , giving customers immediate, detailed visibility into our cloud security posture. How does CSA STAR relate to other standards? The CSA CCM and CAIQ are directly mapped to leading global security standards, including: - ISO/IEC 27001 and 27017 - SOC 2 (AICPA Trust Services Criteria) - NIST and COBIT frameworks This makes CSA STAR an efficient way for customers to understand how a cloud provider’s controls relate to the most recognized security and compliance requirements worldwide.
