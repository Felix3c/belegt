Our ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification demonstrates our commitment to international best practices in information security management and risk mitigation. Access compliance center What is ISO/IEC 27001:2022? ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the leading international standard for information security management systems (ISMS), jointly published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC). It sets out the criteria for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an ISMS — including requirements for assessing and treating information security risks. The standard applies to organizations of all types and sizes, helping them systematically protect sensitive data, comply with legal requirements, and strengthen business resilience. What are the main requirements? ISO/IEC 27001:2022 provides a comprehensive framework for managing information security risks. The key requirements include: Scope & Context : Define the boundaries and applicability of your ISMS in relation to your organization’s context and stakeholders. Leadership & Commitment : Senior management must actively lead and support information security, assigning clear roles and responsibilities. Planning & Risk Management : Identify risks, set information security objectives, and plan risk treatment and continuous improvement. Support : Provide adequate resources, ensure staff are trained and aware, and maintain documented information. Operation : Implement risk treatment plans, manage operations securely, and respond to security events. Performance Evaluation : Regularly monitor, measure, audit, and review your ISMS to ensure effectiveness. Improvement : Continually improve your ISMS based on results from monitoring, audits, incidents, and management reviews. Annex A Controls : The standard includes an updated set of 93 controls (Annex A), covering areas like organizational controls, people controls, physical controls, and technological controls. What changed in the 2022 revision? The 2022 revision of ISO/IEC 27001 introduced several significant updates: Annex A controls streamlined: Controls were reduced from 114 to 93 and regrouped into four categories for clarity. New controls: Additional focus on cloud services, threat intelligence, data masking, physical security monitoring, and web filtering. Improved alignment: Structure now matches other ISO management system standards for easier integration. Emphasis on continual improvement: Enhanced guidance on responding to evolving threats and business needs. Why is ISO/IEC 27001 important for businesses? Information security is essential for every business in today’s digital landscape. ISO/IEC 27001 is globally recognized as the benchmark for building and operating a secure and resilient organization. Reduces risks: Systematically identifies and mitigates information security risks. Builds trust: Demonstrates to customers, partners, and regulators that security is a top priority. Supports compliance: Helps meet legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements. Competitive advantage: Many organizations require suppliers to hold ISO/IEC 27001 certification. Is certification mandatory? Certification is voluntary — any organization can use ISO/IEC 27001 as a framework without being formally certified. However, independent certification by an accredited body provides external assurance and can be a key differentiator in competitive markets, especially when customers or regulators require it. Exoscale has maintained ISO/IEC 27001 certification by an accredited auditor since 2018. How does certification work? To become certified, an organization must: Implement an ISMS in line with ISO/IEC 27001 requirements. Undergo an independent audit by an accredited certification body. Maintain certification through annual surveillance audits and regular improvement. Certificates are typically valid for three years, subject to successful surveillance audits. Important: Using ISO 27001-certified services like Exoscale supports your own compliance journey, but each organization must implement and demonstrate its own ISMS. Does using an ISO 27001 certified cloud provider help my own compliance? Yes, choosing an ISO 27001-certified provider like Exoscale means your cloud workloads run on infrastructure audited for best-practice security management. However, customers remain responsible for the security of their data, applications, and their own compliance. Exoscale’s certifications and documentation make it easier to demonstrate your own due diligence to auditors and regulators. Can I access Exoscale’s certificate and Statement of Applicability? Yes.
