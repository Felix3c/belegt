Register Exoscale - European Cloud Hosting Services Compliance Hds HDS Certification HDS - Hébergement des Données de Santé HDS certification is a mandatory French regulatory requirement for any provider hosting health data. It guarantees that rigorous security, confidentiality, and availability measures are in place to protect sensitive medical information, supporting compliance for healthcare organizations and software vendors. Access compliance center What is HDS? HDS certification (“Hébergeur de Données de Santé”) is the French government’s security framework for companies hosting health data. Required by law for any organization hosting or processing health data on behalf of French healthcare professionals or institutions, HDS ensures that strict measures are implemented for: - Integrity, confidentiality, and availability of health data - Physical and logical security - Data backup and disaster recovery - Traceability and incident management - Business continuity and regulatory compliance Exoscale’s HDS certification can be downloaded from our Compliance Center . FAQ Who needs to be HDS certified? What activities are covered by HDS certification? What qualifies as health data? I’m not based in France. Is HDS still relevant? Who needs to be HDS certified? HDS certification is required for any organization that hosts or processes health data on behalf of healthcare professionals, clinics, hospitals, insurers, pharmacies, mutuals, software publishers, or digital health startups serving the French market. Certification is validated by an independent auditor to ensure compliance with strict requirements for the security and privacy of health information. What activities are covered by HDS certification? HDS covers a range of hosting and IT activities, including: - Providing and maintaining physical data center infrastructure - Managing and operating virtual infrastructure (e.g., cloud services) - Administration and management of information systems containing health data - External backup and secure storage of health data - Business continuity and disaster recovery services Providers must be certified for each specific activity they offer. What qualifies as health data? Health data is any information related to an individual’s physical or mental health, including healthcare services provided and medical records. This includes diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, test results, and other data revealing the health status of a person. The definition is aligned with the GDPR and detailed by the French CNIL. More information is available on the CNIL website . I’m not based in France. Is HDS still relevant? While HDS certification is required by French law, it also demonstrates high standards for health data protection across Europe. For organizations outside France, using an HDS-certified provider like Exoscale is a strong signal to customers, regulators, and partners of your commitment to the highest levels of data security and privacy for sensitive health information—even when not legally required.
