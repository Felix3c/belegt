You want to use AI in your daily clinical work, but you cannot risk patient data ending up in a system you do not control. GreenPT is AI for doctors that runs on infrastructure hosted in the European Union, with open-source models we host ourselves and a positive Data Privacy Impact Assessment behind it. GreenPT is built for clinical work because public AI tools were not. Consumer chatbots send your prompts to servers outside the EU, log conversations on third-party infrastructure, and reserve the right to use parts of your input to improve their models. None of that is acceptable when the prompt contains a discharge summary, a medication list or a description of a real case.
