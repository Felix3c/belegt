GreenPT Privacy
Privacy-first AI
Built from the ground up with privacy by design, and backed by a positive DPIA (Data Privacy Impact Assessment).
Most AI providers quietly train new models on what you type, store your conversations on US servers under the CLOUD Act, and route your data through a long list of third-party processors. GreenPT does none of that. Every chat stays inside the European Union, is never used to train a model, and is never shared with anyone for their own purposes. Every model runs on European infrastructure (Scaleway in France and our EU AI hosting partners in Finland), so no US cloud provider ever sees your data. We can sign a custom Data Processing Agreement tailored to your specific requirements and use case.
Check our Privacy Policy
Privacy isn't a setting. It's the architecture.
EU Data Sovereignty
All data processing happens exclusively within the European Union. Your information never crosses EU borders, ensuring full compliance with EU data protection laws.
No Training Data Usage
We store your chat only for your personal history, and you can manage your own conversations. We never use your data to train our AI models.
End-to-End Privacy
From encryption in transit to secure processing in ISO-certified data centers, your privacy is protected at every step of the AI interaction.
Privacy Infrastructure
We believe in complete transparency about our data practices. You have full control over your data and can exercise all your privacy rights at any time.
Advanced Encryption
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Your conversations are protected from the moment they leave your device.
Security Certifications
The data centres we run on are ISO 27001 certified for information security. GreenPT BV is working towards its own ISO 27001 certification.
Privacy Monitoring
Continuous monitoring and audit trails ensure complete transparency in how your data is handled, with regular compliance reviews and security assessments.
Minimal Data Collection
We only collect the absolute minimum data necessary to provide our AI services.
Your Rights Respected
Full GDPR compliance means you can access, correct, delete, or port your data at any time. Your privacy rights are not just respected but actively protected.
Open Source Models
We use open-source AI models that can be audited and verified. No black box algorithms or hidden data processing.
Privacy FAQ
Where is my data stored?All data is stored and processed exclusively within the European Union. Our platform runs on Scaleway data centers in France. The AI models run on Scaleway infrastructure and with our European AI hosting partners Inceptron, Tensorix and Melious, in Verda's ISO 27001 certified data centers in Finland. Your information never crosses EU borders, ensuring full compliance with GDPR and EU data protection laws.
Do you use my data to train AI models?No. We never use your conversations, documents, or API traffic to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. Every model is self-hosted on GreenPT infrastructure, so your data never leaves our environment.
Can I request a custom Data Processing Agreement?Yes. We sign a standard Data Processing Agreement with every paying customer, and we're happy to negotiate a custom DPA tailored to your specific requirements and use case. Get in touch and our team will work with your legal department directly.
Is my data encrypted?Yes. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and at rest on the underlying ISO 27001 certified storage. Access to your environment is scoped per-account, with audit logging on every administrative action.
Can I delete my chat history?Yes. You can manage and delete your own conversations directly from your account at any time. On account closure, your data is removed from our active systems and purged from backups according to our retention schedule.
Who can access my conversations?Only you, and the people you explicitly invite to your workspace. GreenPT staff have no routine access to customer content; access requires a documented support request from you and is fully logged.
