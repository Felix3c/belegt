Copy Markdown Open GreenPT BV processes personal data on your behalf as a processor, or as a sub-processor when you use GreenPT for your own customers. We engage the authorised sub-processors below. Each one is bound by data-protection obligations no less protective than our Data Processing Agreement (DPA), including its confidentiality, security and no-training obligations. Personal data is hosted only in EU data centres in France and Finland, powered by renewable energy. The one exception is the optional US API endpoint, used only for requests you send to it (see the US tab below). Inference sub-processors Choose an inference region to see the sub-processors that process your requests. EU is selected by default. Set your API base URL to choose where requests run; this switch only changes the information shown below. Your prompts and the generated output pass through Neuralwatt only for requests you send to https://api.us.greenpt.ai . This sub-processor is not used by any other GreenPT service : the default endpoint https://api.greenpt.ai , the EU endpoint https://api.eu.greenpt.ai , GreenPT Apps, GreenPT Chat and everything else are served from the EU sub-processors. Requests to the EU endpoints are never routed to the US endpoint. Sending a request to the US endpoint is your instruction to process it in the United States. We make that transfer subject to a valid transfer mechanism under Chapter V GDPR. Account, billing, email and other platform data stay in the EU whichever endpoint you use. If you do not want requests processed in the US, call the EU endpoints, which is what every client that does not change its base URL already does. GreenPT Search Brave Search is not a sub-processor for data you submit through our services. It powers the search index of our public search site search.greenpt.ai and processes only the search queries entered there. It is not used in the GreenPT API, Speech-to-Text, apps.greenpt.ai or any other GreenPT service, and receives none of their data. For more information about how Brave handles your data, please review their Privacy Policy . Changes to this list Under our DPA we give at least 30 calendar days' written notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor, and update this list the same day. You may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within 28 days of the notice. If we cannot resolve the objection within a further 15 days, you may suspend or terminate the affected services without penalty.
