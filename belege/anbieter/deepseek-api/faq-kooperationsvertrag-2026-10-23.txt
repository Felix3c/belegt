Do you support signing cooperation agreements?

DeepSeek API is provided via a self-service and standardized model. If you have specific offline agreement needs (such as app filing or enterprise vendor onboarding), please fill out a 「Cooperation Agreement Application」ticket and submit the relevant information. We will assist you with the request.

Note: The agreement is a standard framework agreement and cannot be modified. To view it, please log in to the 「Bank Transfer」page to download the template.
