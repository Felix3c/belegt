Confidential Computing keeps your data protected

Confidential Computing inside Model Vault creates a safe and trustworthy execution environment. Memory of in-use data is fully encrypted with keys held inside the hardware, so nobody can look inside.

Any code outside the boundary of your encrypted model vault cannot read the encrypted data, and If verification is needed, seals can be checked on demand to make sure your Model Vault and the data going through it are perfectly safe, and will stay that way.
