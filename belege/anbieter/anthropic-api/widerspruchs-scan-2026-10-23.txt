Inhalt: wörtliche Auszüge aus dem Widerspruchs-Scan Anthropic Claude API (Textfassung). Frage: Sagen die Anthropic-Seiten untereinander oder in der Werbung etwas anderes als das Profil?

A. Werbung: Gedächtnis „on your infrastructure“ (Fall-Kandidat 49)

1. claude.com/platform/api, Abschnitt Managed Agents, Kachel „Memory stores“ (Link „Learn more“ auf platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/memory):
Memory stores Agents remember across sessions, keeping memory files on your infrastructure.

2. Dieselbe Seite, Kachel „Self-hosted sandboxes“:
Keep sensitive files, packages, and services in your own infrastructure, or use a managed provider.

B. Doku: Gedächtnis liegt bei Anthropic, auch mit eigener Sandbox

3. Self-hosted sandboxes: security, „Memory store contents.“:
Memory stores remain hosted by Anthropic, including their version history.

4. Self-hosted sandboxes: memory:
The difference is who materializes the store. On a self-hosted environment your worker, rather than Anthropic's infrastructure, downloads each store into the sandbox and syncs the agent's changes back.
The memory store on Anthropic's side remains the source of truth.

5. Memory (Versionen):
Versions are retained for 30 days after they are written; however, the recent versions of a live memory are always kept regardless of age, so memories that change infrequently might retain history beyond 30 days.

6. API and data retention, Zeile Claude Managed Agents (ZDR: nein):
Sessions are stateful resources; transcripts persist until you delete them. Applies to all Managed Agents sub-features, including
Self-hosted sandboxes

Auf dem eigenen Rechner liegt nur eine Arbeitskopie für die Dauer der Sitzung (Security-Leitfaden: „the worker keeps a working copy under /mnt/memory/ in your sandbox for the session's duration and syncs changes back“).

C. Ohne Widerspruch

7. „Residency On your cloud provider, choose where your data is stored and where requests are processed, with regions in Asia-Pacific, Canada, Europe, and the United States.“ – eingeschränkt auf „On your cloud provider“ (Bedrock, Google Cloud, Foundry); für die direkte API gilt laut Profil „us“ als einziger Speicherort. Kein Widerspruch, aber leicht misszuverstehen.

8. „By default, Anthropic does not use customer data from commercial deployments to train Claude.“ – passt zur Doku „Retained data is never used for model training without your express permission.“

9. „Enterprise Frontier Safeguards Gives eligible customers the privacy of zero data retention (ZDR) along with state-of-the-art safeguards for detecting misuse.“ – die Doku nennt die Ausnahme selbst: „if a chat or session is flagged, Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs for up to 2 years.“ Steht schon im Profil; gleiches Wort-Muster wie Nr. 42, 46, 47, kein eigener Kandidat.
