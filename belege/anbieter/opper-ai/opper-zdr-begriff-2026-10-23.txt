3. pricing, FAQ:
+ On Gateway, zero data retention: only usage metadata, no prompts or responses. On Control Plane, zero data retention is the default: traces including prompts and responses are stored only when you turn tracing on in Rules, for 1 to 30 days, and a project can shorten that down to zero. A model access rule can additionally restrict calls to providers that do not train on or log your data. Claude on AWS Bedrock is zero data retention on pay-as-you-go; the zero-retention routes for OpenAI models on Azure (azure-zdr) and Gemini on Google Vertex (vertexai-zdr) need a signed Enterprise agreement.
+ No. Routes whose provider neither trains on nor logs your content are on every tier and you pick them per call. Control Plane adds the rule that enforces zero data retention for the whole organization or a project, so no call can reach a provider that keeps content. The zero-retention routes for OpenAI models on Azure and Gemini on Google Vertex need a signed Enterprise agreement.

4. security-overview („Last updated on: 2026-10-05“), Abschnitt 2:
For every call, Opper records usage metadata: the model, token counts, latency, status and cost. These records power billing and analytics and are kept for five years.
Enterprise agreements can extend zero retention to the usage metadata too.

5. enterprise, FAQ:
Opper stores only usage metadata such as model, tokens, latency and cost by default, tracing is a rule you turn on with retention from 1 to 30 days that projects can shorten to zero, Enterprise agreements can extend zero retention to the usage metadata, and Opper never trains on your data.
