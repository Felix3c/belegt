Get Started

Overview

Credit-based pricing for all FLUX models including FLUX 3, FLUX.2, FLUX.1, and batch requests. 1 credit equals $0.01 USD.

Simple credit-based pricing: 1 credit = $0.01 USD • Pay per image, or per second for video • Same price for API and Playground

​
Pricing

The following table shows pricing for all FLUX models and their variants:

FLUX 3 Video

Use the FLUX 3 video pricing calculator to calculate the cost of your video. FLUX 3 generates video with synchronized audio and uses per-second pricing — cost scales with clip length, resolution (hd, fhd, qhd, or uhd), and whether you run a full render or a fast draft. Full-render rates are per second of output, by resolution class:
ModehdfhdqhduhdDraft
Text to Video (t2v)
Image to Video (i2v)
Video Continuation (v2v)
Drafts render at hd. Use the published draft rates above to estimate the cost of exploring variants before you enhance the shot you keep. fhd, qhd, and uhd are the same generation finished by the video upsampler at a larger canvas — see delivered dimensions — and are billed at the delivered class. See the FLUX 3 overview for length limits and supported aspect ratios.

FLUX Tools (Video)

FLUX Video Upscale is priced per output megapixel-second: the megapixels per output frame multiplied by the output duration in seconds.
VariantPrice
FLUX Video Upscale (Precise) per megapixel-second¹ ² ³ ⁴
FLUX Video Upscale (Creative) per megapixel-second¹ ² ³ ⁴
¹ One megapixel-second is one megapixel (1,048,576 pixels, i.e. 1024 × 1024) of output resolution for one second of output video. Charges are calculated on the delivered output’s actual frame dimensions and duration: megapixels per output frame × output duration in seconds, with fractional values charged pro rata. Example: upscaling a 10-second clip to 1080p (1920 × 1080 = 1.98 megapixels) is approximately 19.8 megapixel-seconds, i.e. (Precise) or (Creative). ² Charges are based on output only. Input resolution, upscale factor and optional prompts are not charged separately. ³ Rates apply to output at the standard frame rate of 24 frames per second. Where output at a higher frame rate is requested and supported, charges scale proportionally with frame rate (equivalently, the listed rate ÷ 24 per megapixel per frame). Supported input durations, resolutions, upscale factors and frame rates are set out in the documentation. ⁴ Input videos up to 20 seconds. Sources longer than 20 seconds are rejected before processing; they are not truncated, and no charge applies. FLUX Video Edit [fast] is priced per second of output video. The output keeps the source clip’s length.

Fine-tuned Endpoints (Public Beta)

During public beta, fine-tuned FLUX.2 endpoints are billed at the same rate as their base endpoints. For example, a request to flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetuned costs the same as the equivalent flux-2-klein-9b request at the same resolution.
EndpointBase EndpointBeta Pricing
flux-2-klein-9b-kv-finetunedflux-2-klein-9bfrom
Pricing for fine-tuned endpoints may change once out of public beta. See the LoRA inference guide for details on how to call these endpoints.
