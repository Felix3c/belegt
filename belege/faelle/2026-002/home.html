No offsets. No greenwashing. Here's how.

  • European infrastructure, renewable energy

    Our servers run in Europe on renewable energy, with air cooling that uses a fraction of the water most data centers need. Lower footprint, by design, not by offset.

  • The right model for the job

    Not every question needs a heavyweight model. We match each task to the most efficient model that can handle it, cutting energy use by 20-30% with no loss in quality.

  • Leaner models, same results

    Through compression and quantization, we make the AI models themselves smaller and more efficient, so every interaction costs less energy from the start.

  • You can see all of it

    Every conversation shows its real-time energy use and CO₂ output. We don't ask you to trust us, we show you the numbers.

FAQ

How is GreenPT different from ChatGPT or Copilot?

The short answer: same capabilities, fewer compromises. Your data stays in the EU, our servers run on renewable energy, and you can see exactly what each conversation costs in energy.

What does it cost, and is there a trial?

Plans start at €4.50/month for individuals, €17.50/month for professionals and teams. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

View pricing →
Will my team need training to switch from another tool?

No. If your team has used ChatGPT or any chat-based AI, they can use GreenPT immediately. Our Apps feature even removes the need for prompting skills for specific tasks. You can also import your existing chat history from ChatGPT, so everything you have built up carries over.

Where exactly is our data stored?

All data is hosted by Scaleway in France, in datacenters running on 100% renewable energy. Data never leaves the EU. We don't train models on your inputs.

What AI models does GreenPT use?
  • GreenL (Mistral Small 3.2, 24B): fast everyday chat, with Dutch language fine-tuning.
  • GreenR (GPT-OSS 120B): advanced reasoning, vision, and document analysis.
  • Gemma4 (Google Gemma 4, 31B): multimodal, with a 256k context window.
  • Plus additional open-source models available via our AI router.
How does your model hosting work?

We host only models locally on our servers. We don't use any external APIs or services. This means your data never leaves our infrastructure and is not shared with third parties. The models themselves don't store any conversation data.

How does performance compare to proprietary models?

Our models are carefully selected and fine-tuned to deliver performance comparable to proprietary alternatives while being more environmentally conscious and cost-effective.

How do you protect user data?

We implement strict data protection measures and fully comply with GDPR requirements. All data is processed within the EU, and we maintain transparent data handling practices. Our self-hosted model approach means your data never leaves our secure infrastructure.

Read our privacy promise →
How is GreenPT environmentally friendly?

Our infrastructure runs entirely on renewable energy, and we use heat recovery systems to reuse server heat for community heating. While the initial training of the models wasn't performed on green servers, our hosting and inference operations are 100% sustainable.

Read about our sustainability →
What is the environmental impact of AI training?

We acknowledge that the initial training of AI models, including those we use, wasn't performed on green infrastructure. However, we're working with the open-source community to develop more sustainable training practices for future models.

How does your infrastructure reduce environmental impact?
  • Sustainable hosting. All infrastructure is run on European providers that meet strict energy-efficiency and renewable-energy criteria, keeping the overall environmental impact minimal.
  • Efficient model selection. By adjusting model behaviour, GreenPT reduces the required compute by 20–30% while preserving output quality.
  • Technical optimisations. Models are compressed and quantised, resulting in smaller, faster-running versions that consume less electricity.
