Privacy Policy

At Seeweb, we are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy, in agreement with EU regulations (Reg. UE 2016/679 GDPR). We want you to be fully informed about the information we collect, how it is used, shared, protected.

Personal Data Processing

Through the functionalities of the website https://regolo.ai/, Seeweb collects the following types of personal information.

  • Information when you register for an Account: the use of Regolo implies your registration for an account, which may include providing us with your name, surname, e-mail address, phone number, birth date, the date necessary for the billing of the service purchased and the tax treatment related to the Country regulations.
  • User’s contents (Inputs): by using Regolo.ai, you can upload data to the service, that can consist in information, content, files that will be collected for providing you the service requested. Other uses of the input data are not expected or possible.
  • Generated contents (Outputs): the use of Regolo.ai can generate contents that may include your personal data or data related to you directly or indirectly (written information, images and videos, sounds, etc). We collect these data to provide our services. Other uses of the output data are not expected or possible.
  • Communications: e-mail or other communications channels both synchronous and asynchronous between user and provider.
  • Information received automatically: LOGs (including IP address, browser and configuration, place, date and connection time, type of device used), origin of the access (eventual entry); usage data: request type (without the content), duration of the access to the service.
  • Cookies: Technical cookies are to navigate the website and other cookies for the tracking with anonymization. All the related treatments are compliant with Seeweb’s cookie policy detailed below.

These data will be treated in agreement with the above mentioned regulations and with the obligations of privacy expected, following the principles of correctness, lawfulness, transparency and protection of your information.

We treat customer information (1) to provide the service purchased, (2) to respond to any requests of the authority or satisfy other law obligations, (3) for the assessment of responsibilities in case of eventual cybercrime to damage the IT infrastructure, (4) for sending email communications related to products and services similar to those you are already using. If you have any questions or concerns about our disclosed practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at privacy@regolo.ai.

The legal basis for any purpose is the following:

  • Art.6.1 letter f of the GDPR, the legitimate interest of the data controller under certain conditions.
  • Art.6.1 letter a of the GDPR, the data subject has given consent to the data processing.

Cookie Policy

How Seeweb uses cookies

Seeweb Srl is committed to protecting your personal information. A cookie is a text file stored on the computer or mobile device by the server of a website and that only that server will be able to retrieve or read its contents. It contains some anonymous information and allows the website to remember, for example, your preferences or what’s in your cart.

Regolo.ai uses cookies because, in some parts of our site, they are essential, and without them you would not be able to use our services. Cookies also help us to provide you with a better and richer experience, for example, by suggesting products or services of interest to you or in order to interact with social network platforms.

The following sections detail the specific categories of cookies we use, the advertising partners involved, and how we manage your consent.

4. Advertising Partners & Their Trackers

What the data means for you:

  • User‑ID – a pseudonymous identifier created by the partner, not directly linked to your name.
  • Hash‑of‑email – SHA‑256 (or stronger) hash of the email address; irreversible for the partner unless they possess the original email.
  • Conversion ID – an opaque value that lets the partner attribute a conversion (e.g., sign-up, purchase) to a prior ad impression.
  • User‑agent & IP – technical data needed for fraud detection, device‑type classification and geographic approximation.

All data are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and stored by the partner for the period shown above. After expiry the data are automatically deleted or anonymised according to each partner’s retention policy.

5. How We Obtain Your Consent (when needed)

  1. First‑visit banner – As soon as you land on our Regolo.ai domain, a cookie banner appears giving you some choices:
    • “Accept all” – enables analytics and advertising trackers.
    • “Reject non‑essential” – only strictly necessary cookies are set.
    • “Customize” – lets you toggle each category (Analytics, Advertising-X, Advertising-LinkedIn, Advertising-Reddit) individually.
  2. Granular toggles – Within the “Customize” panel you can enable/disable each partner separately. The banner records your explicit “accept” or “reject” action and stores this preference in the cookie_consent record (encrypted, 1‑year lifespan).
  3. Consent logging – Every consent event is logged (date‑time, IP (hashed), user‑agent, selected options) in a secure audit table for compliance evidence.

6. How to Revoke or Modify Your Consent

  • Via the cookie banner – Click the “Cookie preferences” link that appears at the bottom of every page, adjust the settings and hit “Save your preferences”.
  • By email – Send a request to privacy@regolo.ai with the subject “Cookie consent withdrawal”. Include your email address (or the account ID) so we can locate your consent record.
  • Through your account settings – If you are logged‑in, go to Settings → Privacy & Cookies and manage the toggles.

Upon withdrawal, we will immediately stop loading all non‑essential trackers for your browser/device. Historical data already transmitted to the partners cannot be retroactively deleted by us; however we will issue a request to each partner to delete or anonymise the data where feasible (as required under Art. 17 GDPR).

7. Your Rights Under GDPR & ePrivacy (Specific to Cookies)

All requests are answered within pursuant to the deadline imposed by the law. No fee is charged for exercising any of these rights.

8. Data Security

  • Transmission – All data exchanged with partners is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Storage – Personal data retained by Regolo.ai (hashes, consent logs) are stored in encrypted PostgreSQL tables (AES‑256 at rest).
  • Access control – Only a minimal set of authorized personnel (Privacy Office, Security Team) can view raw consent logs; they are subject to regular audit.
  • Incident response – In the unlikely event of a breach affecting cookie‑derived data, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay.

9. International Data Transfers

Any transfer of personal data is done according to the Art. 45 and following of the GDPR.
