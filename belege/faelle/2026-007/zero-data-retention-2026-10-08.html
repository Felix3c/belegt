Zero Data Retention

Your Data Never Stays.
Your Privacy Always Does.

Every request is processed in real time and permanently discarded. No logs, no traces, no compromise. Fully European, fully private.

Generate Your Key Read the Docs

European by Choice, Not by Circumstance

At Regolo, our European identity is not just a geographical fact, but a daily commitment to transparency, regulatory compliance, and sustainability. Our infrastructure operates entirely on data centers located in Europe, ensuring that every data processing and management activity takes place under European jurisdiction.

We believe that keeping data close to users guarantees security, trust, and adherence to European laws. This choice is deliberate and non-negotiable.

GDPR Compliant

We are fully GDPR-compliant, ensuring that every aspect of our service meets the rigorous standards of European data protection law. Your data rights are respected at every level.

EU AI Act Aligned

We have proactively aligned ourselves with the EU AI Act, integrating from the outset procedures and controls that meet the transparency, security, and accountability requirements outlined by the upcoming European regulation on artificial intelligence.

European Jurisdiction

Every data processing and management activity takes place under European jurisdiction. Your data never leaves European soil, from ingestion to inference to response delivery.

We Do Not Keep Your Data.
Period.

We do not retain, log, or reuse any input or output data processed through our models. Every request is handled in real time, and once the response is generated, the data is permanently discarded.

Your data never remains with us: we process it, protect it during transmission, and immediately release it. This zero-retention principle goes beyond GDPR compliance, offering maximum privacy and ensuring that your interactions with Regolo leave no trace.

No Storage

No storage of user prompts or model outputs. Your inputs and the responses generated are never written to disk or persisted in any database.

No Logging

No logging of API calls or content for future analysis. There are no audit trails of your requests, no server logs capturing your data, no records of any kind.

No Reuse

No use of data for training, fine-tuning, or any other secondary purpose. Your data serves one purpose only: delivering the response you requested, then it is gone.
